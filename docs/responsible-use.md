# Responsible use

Fel's Studio helps you make fictional characters, and record, design and clone voices with permission. This page covers
what NPC Forge makes: pictures, 3D models, voices and character cards.

## Keep characters fictional

Do not make a character of a real person: no real name with a real face, and no likeness of someone, famous or not, who has
not agreed. Make a portrait from a photo only if it is your own, or the person in it has agreed to how the character will be
used; keep that agreement. Do not use a character to impersonate, defraud or harass anyone.

Look at what the models draw before you publish it. A model can drift towards a famous face or character whatever the words
say: Z-Image Turbo drew a long-haired pirate captain as a well-known film pirate in 17 of 17 tries, and its note in the
Models card says so. Draw it again, or with another model, when a picture looks like someone real or like a character
someone else owns.

## Voices and consent

NPC Forge's voices come from [Fel's Voice Lab](https://github.com/Fel101/fels-voice-lab). Clone only your own voice, or a
recording whose speaker agreed to it in writing; **Use a recording** asks you to confirm that, and to say where the
recording came from and what it may be used for. A CHARX character card can carry a cloned voice's recording: tick its
sharing box only if the person's permission covers sharing it. Voice Lab's
[responsible-use page](https://github.com/Fel101/fels-voice-lab/blob/main/docs/RESPONSIBLE_USE.md) has more on consent.

## Read the licence badges

Every model has its own licence, and so does what it makes. NPC Forge shows a badge after each model's name, under the
chosen model, on every character (the worst rating of the models that made it) and in the Library:

| Badge | What it means |
|---|---|
| ✓ **Commercial use allowed** | The licence puts no limit on commercial use beyond keeping its notice. |
| ! **Allowed, with conditions** | Read the conditions before you sell, publish or host the work. |
| ? **Not cleared** | The licence and its authors disagree, or it depends on something NPC Forge cannot know. Do not rely on it for work you sell. The built-in Mage-Flow has this badge. |
| ✕ **Non-commercial only** | Research, evaluation or personal use only. Qwen Image 2.1 has this badge. |

The badges say what each model's own page says, read on the date shown; they are not legal advice. NPC Forge's own code is
MIT. [licences.md](licences.md) has every model and how a rating is worked out, and [NOTICE.md](../NOTICE.md) has the
notices to keep.

## Label what you make

Every picture and 3D model NPC Forge saves says inside it whether AI made it, with the app and the models that made it:
PNG text fields, with IPTC's `trainedAlgorithmicMedia` source type on AI-generated pictures, and `asset.extras.fels` in a
GLB ([licences.md](licences.md#provenance-inside-exported-files)). Keep those labels when you publish, and tell people that
the pictures, models and voices were made with AI.

## Add-ons and kids mode

Fel's Studio add-ons are separate downloads, and the core apps' rules apply to everything they do. The add-on made for
adults is separate and for adults only. Altera's kids mode adds guardrails to chat; it is not parental-control software, so
use parental controls and supervision as well.
